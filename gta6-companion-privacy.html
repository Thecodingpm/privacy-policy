Privacy Policy

GTA VI Companion

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Publisher: Muhammmad Ahmad
Privacy contact: ahmadmuaaz292@gmail.com

GTA VI Companion is an unofficial fan companion and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. This policy explains the data practices of the current release.

Data we collect

The current release does not require an account and does not collect precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or payment information. Favorites, completion progress, private map notes, money plans, recent searches, map settings, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

If online account features are enabled in a later release, the account information and user-submitted content required for those features will be identified before collection and covered by an updated version of this policy.

How we use data

Local data is used to provide the map, favorites, progress tracking, private map notes, collections, and companion tools. We do not sell personal information. The current release does not transmit analytics or advertising data.

Sharing and service providers

The current release does not share personal data with analytics, advertising, or social-network providers. Bundled community map and location content is app content, not personal data. Any future service provider that processes personal data will be listed in an updated policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you use Settings → Privacy & data → Delete local app data or uninstall the app. If online accounts are enabled, you will be able to start deletion in the app and through the public account-deletion page. Associated account data will be deleted subject only to limited retention required by law, fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution, which will be explained at the time of deletion.

Security

We limit collection to what is needed for the feature and use platform storage for local data. Future online services must use authenticated access, encrypted transport, and server-side authorization.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can use the current release without creating an account, review this policy in the app, and delete local data from the Privacy & data screen. Contact us for privacy questions or requests at ahmadmuaaz292@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with each material change.
