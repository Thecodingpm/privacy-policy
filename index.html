Project Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Project Atlas is a private beta companion app. This policy explains what information the current beta stores and how it is handled.

Information stored on your device

The current beta stores information locally on your device when you use related features, including a display name or local profile, collection items such as favorites and wishlist entries, manually entered game balance and goals, and local play-group, session, safety, or report data.

Information sent to us

The current release build does not require an account and does not send the information listed above to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics. Development builds may write diagnostic events to local logs for testing; those logs are not sent to us by the release build.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your information. Because the current beta keeps the described data on your device, we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data deletion

You can remove locally stored app data through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Removing app data or uninstalling deletes the local beta data from that device. Data that you have separately copied or exported is not controlled by the app.

Children

Project Atlas is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the beta later adds accounts, online services, analytics, or other data practices, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.

Contact

For privacy questions about this beta, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the Project Atlas Google Play listing.
