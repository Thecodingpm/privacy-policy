Delete your GTA VI Companion account

This page is the public account-deletion path for GTA VI Companion.

Publisher: Muhammmad Ahmad
Privacy contact: ahmadmuaaz292@gmail.com

Current release

The current release is local-first and does not create online accounts. To remove its saved data, open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete local app data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local storage according to the platform’s behavior.

When online accounts are enabled

Sign in to the account you want to remove, then use Settings → Privacy & data → Delete online account. This permanently removes the account and associated user data from the service, except for limited information that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

If you cannot sign in, contact ahmadmuaaz292@gmail.com from the account email and include the minimum information needed to locate the account. Do not send passwords or payment credentials.

Processing

Requests are processed by the authenticated account service. The publisher must connect this page and the in-app action to the production deletion endpoint before enabling account creation or submitting a release that uses online accounts.
